## English (primary)

**Name:** Eksigo

**Subtitle:** Hear the English you missed

**Promotional text:**
Dictate an episode on your computer at night. In the morning your phone plays back the sentences you missed and reviews the words you saved.

**Keywords:**
english,listening,podcast,dictation,learn english,esl,vocabulary,flashcards,ielts,toefl,practice

**Description:**

Eksigo is a podcast player for English learners, and the phone half of a simple daily routine.

At night, on your Mac or Windows computer, you dictate an episode you listened to: play a sentence, type what you heard, and see exactly where your ears let you down. The next day Eksigo on your iPhone brings that back to you.

MISSED SENTENCES
The sentences you got wrong, with what you typed next to what was said. Play each one again, as often as you like, at the speed you need.

YOUR WORDS
Words you saved on the computer come with the sentence and audio you found them in. Review the ones that are due each day; the app spaces them out so you see each word just before you would forget it.

A PODCAST PLAYER MADE FOR LEARNERS
Search Apple's podcast directory or start from shows chosen for learners. Subscribe, download for the commute, change speed from 0.7× to 1.5×, and skip back when you lose the thread.

YOUR PROGRESS
Your streak, the time you listened this week and every episode you played, together with your practice on the computer.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and no tracking. Audio and transcripts stay on your devices. An account is optional: sign in with Apple or Google only if you want your computer and phone to sync.

Listening needs no account. The desktop app is free at eksigo.com.

**What's New in this version:**
- Eksigo now speaks Japanese, Korean, French and Spanish, as well as English and Chinese.
- Changes you make while offline sync as soon as you are back online.
- Clearer wording for counts and for words due far in the future.

## App Review information

**Sign-in required:** No. Leave "Sign-in required" unchecked; there is no demo account, because sign-in is only with Apple or Google.

**Notes for the reviewer** (paste as is):

> Eksigo is the iPhone companion of a free desktop dictation app (Mac and Windows, eksigo.com). Every feature on the phone works without an account: tap "Not now, just listen" on the first screen, then use Discover to find and play podcasts, subscribe, download and change speed.
>
> An account is optional and only syncs text between the computer and the phone (saved words, sentences missed in dictation, listening history). You can sign in with your own Apple ID via Sign in with Apple to see the account screen. A new account has no words or missed sentences yet, because those are created in the desktop app, so the Review tab shows its empty state.
>
> Account deletion: Me → your account → Delete account. It removes the account and its synced data from our server.
>
> Podcast audio is streamed from each publisher's own server. Some publishers still use plain http, which is why NSAllowsArbitraryLoads is set.
